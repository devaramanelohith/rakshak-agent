# Rakshak demo video: shot-by-shot script (2:50)

**One line:** The video is the product, the deck is the argument. A 15-second title and problem card, then the live two-phone call in English on the deployed Cloud Run URL, with the payment screen as the climax at the 1:00 mark and nothing after 2:50.

## 1. Decisions before the first take

**Slides first, or demo first?** A 15-second title and problem card, then the demo. The deck PDF already carries the full argument (problem, gap, architecture, evaluation, business case), so the video should not repeat it. Judges watch dozens of videos; a product that stops a scam in front of their eyes inside the first minute is what they remember. Architecture appears for 20 seconds near the end, over the fraud-desk trace, not as a slide stack at the start.

**The climax rule.** The payment screen is the climax. A live risk meter during a call is something Pixel already shows, and at least five Indian hackathon teams built one this year. What nobody shows is the moment the victim taps Pay and the bank app pauses the transfer, speaks over the caller, shows the mule account's history and tells the family. The video spends 10 seconds on the risk meter and 55 seconds on the hold.

**Main take and backup take.** The main take is the live two-phone flow in English: phone B opens `/call?session=<id>` and rings phone A, the customer (Lohith, `demo-user`) answers, a teammate speaks `script_03` "Cyber Crime Branch money-laundering case (English)", the customer pays. The backup take is the scripted replay on one phone (`Test an incoming call`, `Cyber Crime Branch money-laundering case (English)`, `Auto-play script`), which cannot fail on mic permission or Wi-Fi. Record both. Cut the main take into the video; keep the backup take ready as a drop-in for shots 3 to 5 if the live audio is unusable.

**Language.** The competition is JAPAC-wide, so the headline demo is in English end to end: the scam call is English, Rakshak's whisper and hold voice are English, the agent's reasons are English. No captions are needed for the main take beyond the voice-over captions. Multilingual support (Hindi, Hinglish, Kannada, Tamil, Singapore English) is shown as an optional 20-second beat with the Hindi persona (`demo-user-hi`, Sunita Rao, "Parcel from Taiwan"); see take H and shot 10b. It is a feature line, not the demo language.

**Length.** Target 2:50. The organiser rule is 3 minutes; treat 3:00 as a hard ceiling and never export longer than 2:58.

**Recording location.** Always the deployed URL, never localhost: `https://rakshak-223197895518.asia-southeast1.run.app`. The browser address bar should be visible for at least one second in shot 2 so judges see the Cloud Run domain.

## 2. Recording setup

**Devices.**

- Phone A (the customer, Lohith, `demo-user`, English): Android with Chrome or iPhone with Safari. Opens `/?session=video1&user=demo-user`. Finish the three onboarding taps before recording (name Lohith, trusted contact Priya (sister), language English). Volume at maximum, phone on speaker.
- Phone B (the caller): any phone with Chrome or Safari. Opens `/call?session=video1`. The caller wears wired earphones so phone A's speaker does not feed back into phone B's microphone. The prompter shows `script_03` once phone A's profile is English; confirm the title reads "Cyber Crime Branch money-laundering case (English)".
- Phone C (the trusted contact, Priya): optional third phone or a laptop tab on `/family?user=demo-user`. Its chime is the proof that the alert arrived.
- Laptop: `/desk` in Chrome at 1920x1080, browser zoom 110% so the trace is readable in the final frame.

**Network.** All devices on the same Wi-Fi or one hotspot. Run `scripts/smoke_ws.py https://rakshak-223197895518.asia-southeast1.run.app` ten minutes before recording to confirm the service is warm (MIN_INSTANCES=1) and the hold event arrives in about a second. Check `/api/health` returns `llm: true` and `live: true`.

**Quota.** Text, agents and TTS run through Vertex AI on the deployed service, so there is no daily cap on `gemini-3.8-flash`. The two Live models use the AI Studio key. Do not rehearse more than needed on a free key.

**Screen recording.**

- Phone A: the built-in screen recorder (Android: Quick Settings tile with "Record audio: media and mic"; iPhone: Control Centre screen recording with the microphone on). This captures the screen and, through the microphone, Rakshak's voice from the speaker and the caller's voice from the relay. Keep this recording as the primary audio of the hold moment.
- Laptop: QuickTime (File, New Screen Recording) or OBS for `/desk`.
- Camera: a second phone or a laptop webcam filming phone A in a hand, framed so the hold screen fills two thirds of the frame. This "over the shoulder" shot is what makes the hold feel real; the screen recording is cut in for legibility.

**Audio capture of Rakshak's voice.** Three sources, pick the cleanest in the edit: (1) the microphone track of phone A's screen recording, (2) the camera's microphone, (3) a clean copy from the service: open the case on `/desk`, copy the `spoken_text`, call `GET /api/tts?text=<spoken_text>&voice=Kore` on the laptop and save the returned audio. Use (3) under the screen recording if the room audio is noisy, keeping it synchronised to the moment the hold screen appears.

**Before each take.** Close other tabs on phone A, switch on Do Not Disturb on all phones (ringing in the demo is Rakshak's, not WhatsApp's), confirm the profile card on phone A says Lohith, English, trusted contact Priya (sister), confirm the "Rakshak ON" pill is visible, and reset the session by using a fresh session id (`video2`, `video3`).

## 4. Takes to record

Record in this order; each take on a fresh session id. Phone A stays on `demo-user` (Lohith, English) for every take except take H.

1. **Take A1, main take, full run.** Phone A screen recording + camera + laptop `/desk` recording, all started before phone B taps Call. The teammate reads `script_03` from the prompter. Run shots 3 to 11 without stopping. Keep going even if something is slow; the edit can cut.
2. **Take A2, main take, second run.** Same as A1. Two full runs give two chances at a clean hold moment with good audio.
3. **Take B, backup take, scripted replay.** Phone A only: `Test an incoming call`, `Cyber Crime Branch money-laundering case (English)`, `Auto-play script`, Open UPI pay at the toast (`police.hq.escrow@rakshakdemo`, Rs 5,40,000 pre-filled), Pay. Screen recording with microphone on. This covers shots 4 to 9 if the live audio from A1 and A2 is unusable.
4. **Take C, hold moment close-up.** Camera only, phone A in hand, start recording two lines before the payment demand (line 11), tap Pay, hold still for 25 seconds. Use for shot 7 and the first seconds of shot 8.
5. **Take D, exits.** Phone A on an existing held case: override dialog opened and cancelled; "Talk to Rakshak" with one question and the English voice answer. Record twice.
6. **Take E, fraud desk.** Laptop recording of `/desk` on the case from A1 or A2: slow scroll through policy versus agent, trace, lineage, narrative, referral, the SMS comparison panel. Record at 1920x1080.
7. **Take F, family phone.** Phone C on `/family?user=demo-user` while A1 runs; two seconds of the chime and the alert card.
8. **Take G, benign hard negative (optional, 10 seconds).** Replay "Benign hard negative: daughter's hostel fee" and pay: "Payment sent". Use only if there is time left in shot 10a to say "and when the call is a daughter asking for fees, the payment simply goes through."
9. **Take H, multilingual beat (OPTIONAL, 20 seconds).** Phone A on `/?session=video-hi&user=demo-user-hi` (Sunita Rao, Hindi, trusted contact Priya (daughter)): `Test an incoming call`, `Parcel from Taiwan (CBI digital arrest, Hindi)`, `Auto-play script`, Open UPI pay at the toast (`cbi.verification@rakshakdemo`, Rs 2,50,000), Pay. Screen recording with the microphone on; keep the hold screen and the Hindi voice. This is the only take in Hindi and it is used only for shot 10b. Switch phone A back to `demo-user` afterwards.
10. **Voice-over.** Record all lines in one sitting, in a quiet room, phone or USB microphone 15 cm from the mouth, each line twice. Read at a steady pace; the timings above assume about 150 words per minute.

Title, problem, architecture, evaluation and closing cards: export deck slides 1, 2, 6, 10 and 14 from `submission/Rakshak_Deck.pdf` as PNG at 1920x1080.

## 5. Editing notes

- **Timeline.** 1920x1080, 30 fps. Phone recordings are portrait: place them centred on a navy (#0b1f3a) background, scaled to full height, with the laptop or camera footage filling the frame where the table says so. Never stretch a phone recording.
- **Cuts.** Hard cuts only, no transitions except a 10-frame fade in at 0:00 and out at 2:50. Cut on action: the tap on Pay is the cut between the camera shot and the screen recording.
- **The hold moment.** Keep shot 8 as one unbroken screen recording from the Pay tap to the end of Rakshak's sentence. Do not speed it up. Duck the voice-over fully while Rakshak speaks.
- **Captions.** The main take is entirely in English, so burn in captions for the voice-over only (for judges watching without sound), plus Rakshak's hold sentence in shot 8 so the words are readable over room audio. If shot 10b is used, caption every Hindi line (caller, hold voice, alert) in English. Captions in white on a semi-transparent navy bar at the bottom, 42 px sans-serif, under 12 words each.
- **Labels.** A small corner label on every shot: "Customer phone", "Caller phone", "Family phone", "Fraud desk". A persistent 24 px label bottom-right from 0:15 to 2:30: "Live on Cloud Run: rakshak-223197895518.asia-southeast1.run.app". If shot 10b is used, add "Hindi persona (optional)" to its corner label.
- **Audio.** Voice-over at -16 LUFS, Rakshak's voice and the caller at -20 LUFS, no music under the hold moment. A quiet bed under shots 1, 12 and 13 only, if at all.
- **Timing checks.** The hold screen must appear on the timeline within 1.5 seconds of the Pay tap; if the take was slower, trim the gap. The agent confirmation at 1:40 may be time-compressed with a visible "15 s later" label.
- **Honesty.** Nothing on screen may be mocked up. If a take shows a slow TTS (voice after the screen), keep it; the screen-before-voice ordering is the design.
- **Length.** Hard rule: under 3:00. Export at 2:50; check the exported file's duration before uploading.
- **Export and upload.** H.264, 1080p, 10 Mbps. Upload to YouTube as Public (or Unlisted only if the portal accepts it; the brief says public). Title: "Rakshak: stop the scam at the moment of payment (Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026)". Description: one paragraph, the live URL and the repo. Test the link from a private browser window and from a phone before putting it on the portal.
