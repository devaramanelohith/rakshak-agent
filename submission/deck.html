What Rakshak does

Four moments, one guardian

With the customer's opt-in, Rakshak scores the caller's coercion live and, when Pay is tapped, fuses it with the payee's mule risk to pause the transfer.

1 · Listen

Per-speaker transcription

Live transcript, 8 coercion signals, risk meter and reasons in Hindi, Tamil, Kannada or English.

2 · Whisper

Private warning

At risk 0.85 the customer hears "Rakshak: careful". The caller never does.

3 · Hold + voice

Pay is tapped

Decision in under 100 ms. 30-minute hold, voice barge-in over the caller, payee evidence, typed-amount override.

4 · Alert

Trusted contact + desk

Daughter's phone chimes. Fraud desk gets the case, agent trace and a drafted 1930 referral.

Opt-in, no audio retained, hold expires on its own4 / 14

The agent

An ADK pipeline that can only make the bank stricter

1 risk_investigator

  • lookup_payee_risk: ledger features, band, lineage
  • check_regulated_entity: official-advisory table (I4C, RBI, SPF, MAS, SEBI, NPCI), then Search grounding
  • Writes a 120-word findings note

2 decision_agent

  • place_payment_hold: the human-in-the-loop action node
  • Message written in the customer's language with concrete evidence
Fast path first. The deterministic policy acts in under 100 ms. The agent lands about 6 s later with evidence and a trace.
Policy floor. final = max(agent, policy). The agent confirms or upgrades, never relaxes a hold. A failed agent leaves the policy in force.
Human in the loop. A hold is a pause: one-tap override with a typed amount, voice Q&A, 30-minute expiry. Every step is on the case timeline.

Score = 0.30 fan-in + 0.20 cash-out + 0.15 dormancy + 0.15 FRI + 0.15 complaints + 0.05 new account. Many payers alone is a shop; the combination is a mule.
Every payee card carries a lineage record: source, transactions in window, computed-at time, which fields are registry values, which sinks (Pub/Sub, BigQuery) mirror the stream.
Synthetic ledger in this prototype; no bank data was used. Production: Pub/Sub to BigQuery, scheduled SQL, Firestore lookup.9 / 14

Impact and business case

Who pays, what it costs, how it scales

Who buys

Banks, UPI and PayNow apps that carry the liability under MAS's Shared Responsibility Framework and RBI's customer-protection rules, and the recovery cost of every missed scam.

What they get

Fewer authorised-push-payment losses, an auditable case with a drafted 1930 or ASCom referral per intervention, and a customer who was protected instead of blamed.

Cost per call

Two Live sessions, 10 to 20 flash rubric calls, one cached clip, one ADK run: cents per guarded call at flash-tier prices, versus a ₹2 lakh transfer.

Scale path on Google Cloud

  • Sessions and cases in Firestore (multi-instance, durable)
  • Alerts fanned out through Pub/Sub to SMS, push, dialler
  • Ledger to BigQuery; sql/payee_risk.sql as a scheduled query; materialised for sub-10 ms lookup
  • ADK pipeline on Vertex AI Agent Runtime; profiles in Memory Bank
  • Cloud Run: WebSockets, session affinity, min instances; stateless beyond the live call
Ships as an SDK in the bank app (consent, mic, pay hook) plus the bank-side service; a telco variant streams the call like the Twilio bridge.
Pricing model stated as a hypothesis: per protected account per month, or per intervention12 / 14
Roadmap and limitations

What is real, what is synthetic, what comes next

Honest limitations

  • Ledger and calls are synthetic; no bank partner yet
  • Twilio bridge is one-way: the voice plays on the app, not into the PSTN call
  • Kannada and Tamil voice not reviewed by native speakers
  • Tier 1 on-device is a design, not code
  • Prototype endpoints are open; single always-on instance for the demo
  • The agent's own decision lands about 6 s after the fast path

Next 90 days

  • Real mule features from a bank ledger through sql/payee_risk.sql
  • ADK pipeline on Vertex AI Agent Runtime; profiles on Memory Bank
  • Bidirectional telco stream so the barge-in reaches the call
  • Native-speaker review of Kannada and Tamil
  • Bank pilot behind Firebase Auth and App Check
  • Human-labelled real call corpus for the evaluation
The ask: one bank fraud team and one telco to pilot.
