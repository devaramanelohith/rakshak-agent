# Rakshak deck outline (12 slides)

Audience: AI Builder Cup 2026 judges. Rubric weights: Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation 40%, Problem Alignment & Impact 25%, Innovation & Creativity 25%, UX & Solution Design 10%. Special awards: Best use of Google Cloud AI tools, Most Impactful Solution, Social Choice, Best UI/UX.

**The one rule: the payment screen is the climax.** A live risk meter during a call is something Pixel already shows. What nobody shows is the moment the victim taps Pay and the bank's app pauses the transfer, speaks to them in their language, shows them the mule account's history, and tells their daughter. Every slide and every second of the video builds towards that screen; the risk meter is the setup, not the payoff.

Visual rule for every slide: one screenshot or clip from the running product, one sentence of claim, at most four bullets. Numbers below are the only external numbers to use, with their sources on the slide.
